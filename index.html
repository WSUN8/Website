Before the Team

The Wasatch Range rises more than 11,000 feet above the Salt Lake Valley, a granite wall that has watched over the city since long before a basketball team named for New Orleans jazz music ever set foot here. In 1979, a struggling franchise moved 1,700 miles west to a valley that had never heard of it — and the mountains became part of the story anyway.

Today

A New Era, an Old Range

In 2020, Ryan Smith became the first Black majority owner in NBA history when he purchased the Jazz. His Smith Entertainment Group has since rebuilt the roster around Lauri Markkanen and a wave of young talent, brought major events like the 2023 NBA All-Star Game to Salt Lake City, and revived the franchise's original purple-mountain gradient jersey — the range on the chest, same as the one outside the arena doors.
